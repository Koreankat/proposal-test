Who We Are

Service. Quality. Value. Inspected by the owner, every time.

B.B. Roofing & Exteriors was founded in May 2021 by Brandon Yniguez, a U.S. military veteran. Brandon started the company with one principle: do the work the way we were trained to do it. Show up. Do what you said you would do. Leave the site clean. Stand behind the result.

In December 2024, the Better Business Bureau of Greater Houston and South Texas accredited B.B. Roofing with an A+ rating. Today, we work across Greater Houston's North and West suburbs from our base in Spring, Texas.
