Meet the Brothers

“We realized we could do a better job giving customers a proper roof that wouldn’t need repairs after just a couple years.”

Co-Founder & Owner

Andre Afsharian

Chris Afsharian Co-Founder · since 2011

Andre and Chris Afsharian started Specialist Roofing in 2011 because they kept getting called to fix roofs that other contractors had installed badly. The repair work was steady, but the pattern was frustrating: the same neighborhoods, the same workmanship problems, year after year.

“We found ourselves getting called to repair roofs that were poorly installed by other roofing companies. That’s how we decided to start installing new roofs, to do the job correctly from the start.”

Fifteen years later, the brothers run a 25-person crew with offices in Burbank, Orange, and Long Beach. They renew GAF Master Elite every cycle, hold Owens Corning Platinum status, and run Polyglass PG Quantum on commercial low-slope work.

Same family. Same standard. Different decade.
