Israel Elgamil opened Park Heights Roofing in 1999 with a simple bet: that homeowners in Pikesville and Baltimore would pay a fair price for an honest roof, done right the first time. 27 years later, 552 Google reviews and 9 award badges say it worked.

Park Heights Roofing specializes in slate roof maintenance, new shingle roofs (including Lifetime systems), and commercial-grade seamless aluminum gutters and gutter guards.
