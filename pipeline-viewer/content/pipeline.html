02 · The Process

The 13 stages

Each stage feeds the next. Nothing is assumed. Everything is derived from the contractor's real data. 13 specialized agents, each doing one thing perfectly, hand the work down the line like a Toyota assembly line for websites.

Phase 1 Discovery
01

Intake

Drop in a contractor's URL. The factory sets up the project folder, scaffolds the build, and kicks off the run. No forms. No client questionnaires.

02

Research

It maps the contractor's local market and identifies their top 3-5 competitors. Studies what's ranking, what's converting, and where the gaps are — the openings the new site will exploit.

Phase 2 Capture
03

Asset Scrape

Pulls real assets from the contractor's existing site — logo, owner photo, team photos, project gallery, badges, certifications, service-area cities. Their stuff, not stock.

04

Real Review Evidence

Fetches verified Google + Facebook reviews direct from the platforms. The 5-star ratings on the new site come from the contractor's real customers — not "lorem ipsum five stars."

Phase 3 Brand DNA
05

Brand Audit

Extracts brand colors from the contractor's logo + live-site CSS. Extracts typography from the same. The new site stays them — same brand DNA, executed at agency quality.

06

Existing-Site Intelligence

Scores the contractor's current site on 12 axes — what's working, what's worth keeping, what to replace. Strong site? Preserve the spine. Blank slate? Build clean from scratch.

Phase 4 Strategy
07

SEO + Content

Keyword research on the contractor's local market, sitemap planning, and conversion-focused copy for every page. Headlines that hook. Bullets that close. CTAs that convert.

08

Design Intelligence

Picks the visual style based on the contractor's brand voice — owner-led residential, premium-family, or commercial-industrial. The right vibe for their specific market and customer.

09

Creative Direction

Chooses the hero composition, color motifs, and character mascot — generating one with AI if the contractor doesn't have one yet. Every section gets a "signature move" so the site feels designed, not assembled.

Phase 5 Build
10

Build

Assembles the site. One file, ~2,500 lines of polished HTML/CSS/JS. Every section coherent. Mobile-first. Accessible. Conversion-tuned. Ready to open in a browser the moment it ships.

Phase 6 Quality
11

QA Audit

Audits against 30+ quality gates: typography matches the contractor's brand, decorations visible, real assets present, no AI-slop language, mobile responsive, every CTA works, every link resolves.

12

Auto-Iterate

If anything fails QA, the factory fixes it automatically — up to 3 iterations. Every fix is documented. Most builds ship clean on iteration 1 or 2.

Phase 7 Sales-Ready
13

Proposal

Generates a $10K-style sales proposal. Branded cover. Embeds the contractor's live site in a laptop + phone mockup. Includes competitive analysis, the $1M case study, sales psychology that closes. Forward it to the lead — they sign.
