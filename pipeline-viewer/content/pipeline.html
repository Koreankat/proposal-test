02 · The Process

The 13 stages

Each stage feeds the next. Nothing is assumed. Everything is derived from your real data. 13 specialized agents, each doing one thing perfectly, hand the work down the line like a Toyota assembly line for websites.

Phase 1 Discovery
01

Intake

You give us your URL. We set up your project folder, scaffold the build, and start the run. Zero forms to fill out.

02

Research

We map your local market and identify your top 3-5 competitors. We study what's ranking, what's converting, and where the gaps are — the openings YOUR site will exploit.

Phase 2 Capture
03

Asset Scrape

We pull your real assets from your existing site — logo, owner photo, team photos, project gallery, badges, certifications, service-area cities. Your stuff, not stock.

04

Real Review Evidence

We fetch your verified Google + Facebook reviews direct from the platforms. The 5-star ratings on your new site come from real customers — not "lorem ipsum five stars."

Phase 3 Brand DNA
05

Brand Audit

We extract your brand colors from your logo + live site CSS. We extract your typography from the same. Your new site stays YOU — same brand DNA, executed at agency quality.

06

Existing-Site Intelligence

We score your current site on 12 axes — what's working, what's worth keeping, what to replace. Strong site? We preserve the spine. Blank slate? We build clean from scratch.

Phase 4 Strategy
07

SEO + Content

We do keyword research on your local market, build your sitemap, and write conversion-focused copy for every page. Headlines that hook. Bullets that close. CTAs that convert.

08

Design Intelligence

We pick your visual style based on your brand voice — owner-led residential, premium-family, or commercial-industrial. The right vibe for your specific market and customer.

09

Creative Direction

We choose your hero composition, your color motifs, your character mascot (we generate one if you don't have one yet, using AI). Every section gets a "signature move" so the site feels designed, not assembled.

Phase 5 Build
10

Build

We assemble your site. One file, ~2,500 lines of polished HTML/CSS/JS. Every section coherent. Mobile-first. Accessible. Conversion-tuned. Ready to open in a browser the moment it ships.

Phase 6 Quality
11

QA Audit

We audit against 30+ quality gates: typography matches your brand, decorations visible, real assets present, no AI-slop language, mobile responsive, every CTA works, every link resolves.

12

Auto-Iterate

If anything fails QA, we fix it automatically — up to 3 iterations. Every fix is documented. Most builds ship clean on iteration 1 or 2.

Phase 7 Sales-Ready
13

Proposal

We generate a $10K-style sales proposal. Branded cover. Embeds your live site in a laptop + phone mockup. Includes competitive analysis, the $1M case study, sales psychology that closes. Forward it to your prospect — they sign.
